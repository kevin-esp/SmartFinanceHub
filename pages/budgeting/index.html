Budgeting & Saving

Learn how to control your money instead of letting your money control you.

What is a Budget?

A budget is a financial plan that helps you track your income, expenses, savings, and financial goals. Budgeting allows you to make informed spending decisions and prepare for future expenses.

Why Budgeting Matters

  • Know exactly where your money is going.
  • Reduce unnecessary spending.
  • Build an emergency fund.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Reach financial goals faster.

The 50 / 30 / 20 Rule

One popular budgeting strategy recommends:

  • 50% Needs
  • 30% Wants
  • 20% Savings & Investments
